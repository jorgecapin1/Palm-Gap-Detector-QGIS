about=Palm Gap Detector analyzes georeferenced oil palm points in projected coordinate systems and detects probable missing palms by finding large spacing jumps within real planting rows. The tool is designed for oil palm plantations and assumes a triangular/staggered planting system. It supports optional lot polygons, multiple optional exclusion layers for roads, streams, drains, buildings or non-plantable areas, confidence labels, sequence and cluster indicators, and export to temporary memory layer, GeoPackage or Shapefile. Detected gap points that fall inside an exclusion layer or its buffer are omitted from the output. This version includes QGIS 3.x and QGIS 4.x / Qt5 and Qt6 compatibility updates. For best results, use vector layers in a projected CRS with meter units.
